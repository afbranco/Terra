# Publications

## How to cite

If you use Terra in your work, please cite the main article:

> Adriano Branco, Francisco Sant'Anna, Roberto Ierusalimschy, Noemi Rodriguez and Silvana
> Rossetto. **Terra: Flexibility and safety in wireless sensor networks.** *ACM Transactions on
> Sensor Networks*, 11(4):59:1-59:27, September 2015.
> [doi:10.1145/2811267](https://doi.org/10.1145/2811267)

```bibtex
@article{branco2015terra,
  author  = {Branco, Adriano and Sant'Anna, Francisco and Ierusalimschy, Roberto and
             Rodriguez, Noemi and Rossetto, Silvana},
  title   = {Terra: Flexibility and Safety in Wireless Sensor Networks},
  journal = {ACM Transactions on Sensor Networks},
  volume  = {11},
  number  = {4},
  pages   = {59:1--59:27},
  month   = sep,
  year    = {2015},
  doi     = {10.1145/2811267}
}
```

The language of Terra, Céu-T, is derived from Céu:

> Francisco Sant'Anna, Noemi Rodriguez, Roberto Ierusalimschy, Olaf Landsiedel and Philippas
> Tsigas. **Safe system-level concurrency on resource-constrained nodes.** In *Proceedings of the
> 11th ACM Conference on Embedded Networked Sensor Systems (SenSys '13)*, Rome, November 2013.
> [doi:10.1145/2517351.2517360](https://doi.org/10.1145/2517351.2517360)

```bibtex
@inproceedings{santanna2013ceu,
  author    = {Sant'Anna, Francisco and Rodriguez, Noemi and Ierusalimschy, Roberto and
               Landsiedel, Olaf and Tsigas, Philippas},
  title     = {Safe System-level Concurrency on Resource-Constrained Nodes},
  booktitle = {Proceedings of the 11th ACM Conference on Embedded Networked Sensor Systems},
  series    = {SenSys '13},
  year      = {2013},
  doi       = {10.1145/2517351.2517360}
}
```

More about Céu at [ceu-lang.org](http://www.ceu-lang.org/).
